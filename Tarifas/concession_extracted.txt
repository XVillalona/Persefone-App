The Parties entered into Annex B1.0 – Punta Cana, Agreed Services and Charges to the Standard Ground Handling Agreement (SGHA) dated June [*] 2025, with effect from October 27, 2024, for the provision of Ramp Services to the Carrier at Punta Cana International Airport (PUJ).
The Handling Company grants to the aforementioned Carrier the following Special Concessions:
Special Concession 1: Prepayment Terms
The Handling Company grants the Carrier a concession from the standard one-month prepayment requirement. Instead, the Carrier will operate under a semi-monthly prepayment structure, with two (2) payments per month.
The Handling Company will issue a Proforma Invoice covering service prices for each half- month period (i.e., the 1st to 15th and 16th to end of the month), including airport fees, and services.
The effectiveness of all Special Concessions described herein is conditioned upon the Carrier’s continued compliance with the established semi-monthly prepayment schedule. Prepayments must be received:
For the first period of each month: no later than the 25th of the prior month (e.g., payment for operations from September 1 to 15 must be received by August 25).
For the second period of each month: no later than the 10th of the current month (e.g., payment for operations from September 16 to 30 must be received by September 10).
A grace period of five (5) calendar days from the applicable due date shall apply. During such grace period, payments may still be made without triggering suspension of the Special Concessions.
Failure to meet these payment deadlines, including the expiration of the grace period will constitute a non-compliant period, and all concessions will be suspended for the full duration of the affected operational period (i.e., from the 1st to the 15th, and/or from the 16th to the end of the month), until full compliance is reestablished and outstanding payments are received.
Special Concession 2: Basic Ramp Service Package
The Basic Ramp Service Package cost established in Table 1 of the Ramp Service Tariff for Punta Cana dated October 30, 2022, will not apply. By default, Arajet will be granted a flat rate of USD$500.00 per turnaround operation for 737-MAX 8 aircraft operating through the commercial
terminal. A turnaround operation is defined as one inbound flight (arrival) followed by one outbound flight (departure) for the same aircraft registration. Total turnaround time (arrival and departure) included in this concession is 70 minutes, as stated in Table 4 of the Ramp Service Tariff for Punta Cana dated October 30, 2022.
Special Concession 3: Concession Pricing & Volume-Based Adjustment 
All services under this agreement, including the Basic Ramp Service Package, will initially be invoiced using the concession prices described in this document.
However, if in any given month the Carrier, Operates fewer than 226 flights, and
the rolling four-month average also falls below 226 flights per month, then the Handling Company reserves the right to issue an adjustment invoice for that month.
This invoice will retroactively apply the standard tariff rates in the SGHA and Ramp Service Tariff for Punta Cana dated October 30, 2022, in place of the concession prices for all services rendered during that month.
The adjustment invoice will be issued concurrently with the next scheduled prepayment invoice and will be payable under the same terms.
Special Concession 4: Water and Lavatory Services
The Water and Lavatory Services cost established in Table 5 of the Ramp Service Tariff for Punta Cana dated October 30, 2022, will not apply. By default, Arajet will be granted a flat rate of USD$50.00 for both services combined.
Special Concession 5: Mini Baggage Truck Rate for Connecting Baggage
For flights carrying connecting passengers, the Handling Company will charge the Mini Baggage Truck rate of USD$25.00 per segment only for the transportation of short connecting baggage (less than 60 minutes), regardless of equipment used, in accordance with Table 5 of the Ramp Services Tariff. The focus is solely on the type of transfer (short-connection baggage), not the equipment configuration, and is established as a differentiated operational rate in accordance with Table 5 of the Ramp Services Tariff.
This concession will only apply to baggage connections moved within 60 minutes of the originating flight's arrival. Movements performed after 60 minutes will be considered outside the short-connection threshold and charged according to the standard equipment rates as published. For movement performance exceeding 60 minutes, this service shall not be required unless expressly requested by the Carrier. 
Special Concession 6: Complimentary Crew Bus Service
For each scheduled operation under this agreement, the Handling Company will provide one (1) complimentary free of charge crew transfer. This service will be offered per turnaround and limited to the crew associated with the corresponding flight.
Special Concession 7: 
Type 2 Cabin Cleaning Service – Fixed Rate (737-MAX 8 Only) The Handling Company will provide Type 2 Cabin Cleaning Service at a flat rate of USD$85.00 per service, exclusively for operations performed with 737-MAX 8 aircraft. This rate
replaces the corresponding standard fee listed in the Ramp Services Tariff for Punta Cana and applies solely to flights covered under this agreement.
To ensure feasibility within operational constraints, the following conditions will apply:
For turnaround operations with a scheduled ground time of 70 minutes or less, the applicable service will default to Type 1 cleaning, given that Type 2 procedures (e.g., vacuuming) cannot be completed within Arajet’s defined 12-minute cabin servicing window.
Type 2 cleaning will only be offered for operations with a scheduled ground time exceeding 70 minutes, allowing sufficient time for execution without impacting the turnaround schedule.
-No use of Ground Power Unit (GPU) will be included or assumed under this cleaning service unless separately requested and billed. Should the Carrier require Type 2 cleaning for shorter turns, a review and potential rate adjustment may be considered depending on GPU usage, time constraints, or alternative procedures agreed upon by both parties.

Explanatory Note on Flight Volume Adjustment Mechanism
The concession pricing granted under this agreement is contingent upon the Carrier maintaining operational volumes aligned with expected levels.
To retain concession pricing, the Carrier must maintain a rolling four-month average of at least 226 flights per month.
If the abovementioned thresholds are missed, an adjustment invoice will be issued charging the difference between standard tariff rates and concession rates for that month.
This invoice will be delivered together with the next regular prepayment invoice and due on the same terms.

Paragraph II. The Special Concession granted herein shall be valid for a term of three (3) years, from June 1st, 2025, to May 31st, 2028. Either Party may terminate this concession by providing six (6) months’ prior written notice to the other Party.
Paragraph III. The Carrier agrees not to disclose the existence of this Special Concession. Failure to comply will immediately render the concession without effect, unless such information has been made public anywhere by anyone other than the Carrier or any other person who derived the information directly from the Handling Company, but shall exclude the scenarios where the Carrier can demonstrate: (a) the existence of the special concession  became lawfully known or available from a source other than the Carrier without breach of this Annex; (b) was within, or later falls within, the public domain without breach of this Annex; (c) was publicly disclosed with the prior written approval of the Handling Company; or (d) was disclosed pursuant to a requirement or demand of a lawful governmental or judicial authority, but only to the extent required by operation of law, regulation or court order.
